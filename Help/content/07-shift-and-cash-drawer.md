# Shift and Cash Drawer

NLI-POS does not provide a shift-opening, drawer-opening, cash-count, or shift-closing workflow. The application does not record an opening balance or a counted closing balance.

## Review recorded sales

1. Select **Reports** > **Daily Sales**. **Expected result:** The Daily Sales report opens.
2. Choose the correct **Office** and **Date**. **Expected result:** The report is set to the office and date you need to review.
3. Select **Filter**. **Expected result:** The report shows the sales recorded for the selected office and date.
4. Compare the report with your store's separately recorded drawer count. **Expected result:** You can review NLI-POS sales, but the report does not represent a cash count or drawer reconciliation.

Use your store's separate cash-handling process to open, count, and close the drawer.
