# Making a Sale

Use **Order** to create an order, add products, record one or more payment methods, and save the sale.

## Enter the order

1. Select **Order** in the navigation. **Expected result:** The Orders list opens.
2. Select **New Order**. **Expected result:** The New Order form appears.
3. Choose the correct **Office** and **Order Date**. **Expected result:** The order is associated with the selected office and date.
4. Select **Search Customer**, search by name, customer ID, email, or mobile number, and choose the matching customer. **Expected result:** The customer's name and class appear on the order.
5. If the customer is not listed, select **New Customer**, complete the required fields, and save the customer. **Expected result:** The customer record is saved and can be selected on the order.
6. Choose a **Category**: **Regular**, **Promo/Package**, or **Freebie**. **Expected result:** The product list reflects the selected category.
7. Choose a **Product**. For a package, choose **Promo Product or Combination (if applicable)**. **Expected result:** The selected item and available price appear.
8. Enter **Quantity** and select **Add to Cart**. **Expected result:** The item appears in **Cart**, and the amount due updates.
9. Repeat product selection and **Add to Cart** for each item.

## Take payment and place the order

1. Under **Payment**, choose a **Method** and enter the payment **Amount**. **Expected result:** The payment row shows the selected tender and amount.
2. Enter a **Ref No.** when the payment method requires a reference. **Expected result:** The reference appears on the payment row.
3. Add another payment row if the customer uses more than one method. **Expected result:** Each tender appears as a separate payment line.
4. Choose the required **Sales By** person. Choose a **Sales Source** or enter **Notes** when needed. **Expected result:** The order contains the selected salesperson and any optional sale details.
5. Check that the payment total is at least **Total Amount Due**. **Expected result:** The payment covers the displayed amount.
6. Select **Place Order** and confirm the prompt. **Expected result:** The order is saved, a receipt is available from its details, and stock is reduced for the items sold.

## Discounts, taxes, and charges

The order form does not provide a general manual discount or tax-entry field. Product prices and package pricing come from the product setup. A payment method may have a configured service charge; review the charge shown on the payment row before placing the order.

For receipt and reprint steps, see [Receipts](/Help/receipts).
