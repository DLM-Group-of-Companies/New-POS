Troubleshooting and FAQ

Use the steps below to resolve common validation messages. If an issue continues, note the page, action, and exact message and contact your administrator.

Sale validation messages

Message or problem What to do
“Please select a customer.” Choose a customer before adding an item to the cart.
“Please select a product.” Choose a product before selecting Add to Cart.
“Please select a promo product or combination.” For a Promo/Package sale, choose Promo Product or Combination (if applicable).
“Price is not set for this product.” Ask an administrator to review the product price for the selected customer class and country.
“Not enough stock for …” Check available stock for the product or package component. Reduce the quantity or ask inventory staff to correct the stock record.
Payment is less than the order total. Add or correct payment rows until the total is at least Total Amount Due.
“Please select a Sales Person before placing the order.” Choose a person in Sales By, then place the order again.

Other common problems

  • Required fields on Customer Registration: Complete the fields marked required, including Customer Class, Office, First Name, Last Name, Gender, Birthday, Civil Status, and Country.
  • A page is missing or access is denied: Ask an administrator to check that your account is active and has the required role and office access.
  • A report is empty: Check the selected office and date or month, then select Filter again.
  • Receipt email reports an error: Check the recipient address and ask your administrator whether receipt email service is available. Use Print as an alternative.
  • A page keeps loading or results do not update: Check your network, refresh the page, and repeat the action. If it continues, report the page and message to your administrator.

Frequently asked questions

Can I put a transaction on hold and finish it later?

No. NLI-POS does not have a hold-and-recall workflow. Follow your store's procedure for an interrupted checkout.

Can I record a refund or product return?

No. The available order action is Void Order. Follow your store's separate return and refund process.

Where do I open or close the cash drawer?

NLI-POS does not track drawer counts or shift opening and closing. Use your store's cash-handling procedure.

Can I add a discount or tax to an order?

The order-entry screen has no general discount or tax field. Product prices and configured package pricing determine the displayed amounts.
