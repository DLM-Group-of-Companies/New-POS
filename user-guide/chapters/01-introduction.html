Chapter 01

Introduction

NLI-POS is a browser-based point-of-sale and back-office application. You can enter customer orders, record payments, maintain customer and product records, track stock by location, and review sales information.

Your account's assigned roles and office access determine which records and pages you can use. Ask your administrator which access applies to your work.

Who uses NLI-POS

  • Cashiers and customer-service staff enter orders, record payments, and look up customers when they have the required access.
  • Store managers review sales and stock information. The application does not define a separate Manager role; an administrator assigns the roles and office access that a manager needs.
  • Inventory staff review stock, record stock changes, move stock between locations, and review inventory transactions.
  • Accounting staff review sales, payment, and report information.
  • Administrators manage users, offices, products, inventory, payment methods, lookup lists, audit logs, and data utilities.

The roles configured in the application are Admin, CS, Accounting, and Inventory. The screens available to you can also depend on your assigned office or offices.

What you can do

  • Enter and review customer orders and payments.
  • Print or email a receipt from an order.
  • Void an order. Voiding restores the sold stock; a separate return or refund workflow is not available.
  • Maintain customers, products, prices, and inventory, when your access allows it.
  • Review sales reports and the dashboard.

The application does not provide a cash-drawer shift workflow or a hold-and-recall workflow. See Voids, Refunds, and Returns, Holding and Recalling Transactions, and Shift and Cash Drawer for details.
