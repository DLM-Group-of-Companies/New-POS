Products and Inventory

Product and stock setup affects checkout. Use only the offices and locations assigned to your work. The Settings menu is visible to administrators; inventory access may also depend on your role and office access.

Add or update a product

Figure: Search the Products list and open a product to view or edit; product records are concealed.

  1. Open Settings > Products. Expected result: The Products list opens.
  2. Select Add New to create a product, or choose the edit action beside a product. Expected result: The product form opens.
  3. Choose the Product Class, Product Type, and Category. Expected result: The product is assigned the appropriate grouping.
  4. Enter the Product Code, Product Name, and any description. Expected result: The product identity is filled in.
  5. Choose the price country and enter the applicable price fields, such as Regular, Standard Dist, Staff, BPP, or Med Pack. Expected result: The product has the prices needed by its customer classes.
  6. Set Active, Freebie Available, and Staff Available as appropriate. Expected result: Product availability matches the store's setup.
  7. Select Save. Expected result: The product or product changes are saved.

The Products list includes search and export or print actions. Promo/Package products may have combinations that identify their component products and quantities.

Review and record stock

  1. Open Settings > Inventory.
  2. Choose Warehouse, Stockroom, or Offices in the inventory navigation. Expected result: The selected location's inventory list appears.
  3. Review the product's stock quantity and minimum level.
  4. To record stock, select Add New, choose the Location and Product, enter Stock Quantity and Minimum Level, and add Remarks if needed. Expected result: The stock entry is ready to save.
  5. Select Save. Expected result: The stock record is saved for the selected location.

Transfer stock

  1. Open the relevant inventory list and choose the product's transfer action. Expected result: The Stock Transfer form opens.
  2. Choose the From and To locations. Expected result: The transfer identifies the source and destination.
  3. Enter the Quantity and any Remarks. Expected result: The transfer contains the quantity to move and any note.
  4. Select Transfer. Expected result: Stock moves between locations and the transfer is recorded.

Locations, transactions, and conversions

  • Use Locations to add or edit a location's Name, Location Type, and optional Office.
  • Use Transactions to review recorded stock movements by date, type, product, location, quantity, and user.
  • Use Conversion to manage rules connecting a From Product, To Product, and conversion Quantity.
